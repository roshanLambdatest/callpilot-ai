# CallPilot v4.3 Demo

1. Ensure CallPilot appears in the macOS menu bar.
2. Open normal Chrome and join a Google Meet.
3. Verify a reminder appears that CallPilot is ready.
4. Menu bar → **Start Call**.
5. Have the other participant ask: “Does your platform support testing IVR systems and DTMF inputs?”
6. Verify local transcription triggers a grounded answer in the floating CallPilot panel.
7. Ask an unsupported question such as: “Is the product NASA-certified for spacecraft AI?” Verify CallPilot does not invent support.
8. Leave/close the meeting and verify CallPilot stops automatically after the grace period.
