# DEMO DATA — Solutions Engineering POC Playbook

> Fictional demo content for AI Call Copilot.

A typical technical POC lasts one to two weeks after prerequisites are ready. The recommended flow is discovery, success-criteria agreement, environment setup, scenario execution, blocker review, and final readout.

## Discovery questions

For agent testing opportunities, confirm the channel being tested, whether the agent is chat or voice, how the customer exposes a test endpoint, expected traffic, data sensitivity, success criteria, and the most important failure modes.

## Success criteria

Good POC criteria are measurable. Example criteria include: generate at least 20 useful scenarios from an uploaded workflow, execute voice tests against a sandbox number, identify at least three meaningful failure cases, and produce an exportable run summary.

## Common blocker

If the customer asks whether a capability is supported but the source material does not verify it, the SE should not guess. Capture the question, verify with the product team, and follow up in writing.
